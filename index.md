Hi, my name is Matheus Araujo! 

I am a biologist studying uncertainty in biodiversity big data and the mechanisms shaping global biodiversity patterns, with a focus on developing quantitative approaches in macroecology.

I am currently a PhD candidate at the Universidade Federal de Goiás (Brazil), under the supervision of  [Prof. Dr. Thiago Rangel](https://scholar.google.com.br/citations?user=EHbuZpYAAAAJ&hl=pt-BR&oi=ao). Part of my research involves developing methods and softwares to quantify uncertainty in species distribution ranges, niche estimates, and phylogenetic measures. In addition, I investigate biodiversity patterns in [environmental space](https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.70008) under the co-supervision of [Prof. Dr. Catherine Graham](https://scholar.google.com.br/citations?user=WUFF6hMAAAAJ&hl=pt-BR&oi=ao), during my internship at the Swiss Federal Institute for Forest, Snow, and Landscape Research (WSL) in Birmensdorf, Switzerland.

I am open to postdoctoral opportunities starting in early 2027.
